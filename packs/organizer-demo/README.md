# Organizer demo

A life organizer: six areas of a life, tasks with due dates, routines that come back, and bigger goals broken into steps.

- Categories: Work, Learning, Home, Health, People, Fun.
- Its word for a star-dot: “task”.
- The starter board: 42 tasks.
- Rules: the defaults (a hierarchy, kites on every star, questions off).

Open it from **States ▾ › New from a blueprint…**. Its content is original, written for Star Lattice.

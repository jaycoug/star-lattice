# Trivia night

A game: six rounds of twenty questions, a few on the field at a time. Answer three right in a round and a goal question twinkles; answer it to win the round.

- Categories: Science, History, Geography, Arts & books, Sports, Food & drink.
- Its word for a star-dot: “question”.
- The starter board: 120 questions, 120 with questions.
- Rules: kites central, stardots independent, perStar 12, deploy auto, questions on, goalAfter 3, shuffleChoices on, shuffleQuestions on.

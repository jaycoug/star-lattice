A campaign notebook: six factions of a made-up coast, their people and places, notes, and how they feel about each other.

- Categories: The Harbor Guild, The Lamplighters, Saltmarsh Folk, The Drowned Choir, House Vey, The Wild Coast.
- Its word for a star-dot: “entry”.
- The starter board: 38 entries.
- Rules: the defaults (a hierarchy, kites on every star, questions off).

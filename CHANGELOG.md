# Changelog

The code used to mark its history in comments (`// round 9 (Y2): …`). Those markers were taken out in stage 5. This file is where the history lives now, one line per round or stage. Letter codes such as Y2 or AA7, where they remain, are spec numbers from those rounds.

## The design version and the play version

- **The stars' look (2026-11-12, to compare):** ⚙ Design › Look › **Kite finish**: Flat (as before, the default), **Crystal** (each kite cut into two facets lit from the upper left, a ridge and a bright rim) or **Glass** (each star lit from its center, fading toward the tips, with a bright rim); both put a small glowing core with a four-point flare at each star's center, and give stars without kites the same, fainter. Only kites in plain color get it: a kite showing a design (painted or cut out) is its design, untouched. ⚙ Design › Fun › **Starry background** (off by default, designer only): two depths of twinkling stars fill the whole sky behind the comets and the board, drifting slower than the board as it pans; with Nebula fog on, the fog dims them past the outermost star (and drops its own flecks).
- **P9e, Streaks and marks (2026-11-12):** a switch in ⚙ Design › Fun, off by default. In Play mode, right answers in a row are a streak (a wrong one ends it): “✦ n in a row” shows by the map from 2, and from 3 the lit star-dots glow and twinkle, faster from 5 and from 10. Five marks, each earned once a session with a message and a badge by the map: ✧ First light (the first star-dot ignited), ✦ Five in a row, ✺ A whole star (every star-dot of a star ignited, or a category complete), ◐ Halfway, ★ Every star lit. A new session starts them over; Edit mode earns none.
- **P9e, Nebula fog (2026-11-12):** a switch in ⚙ Design › Fun, off by default. In Play mode, a dark haze with slowly drifting nebula clouds (violet, teal, rose) and faint twinkling flecks covers the empty sky past the outermost star, its edge soft so the next ring's positions show through. Each star deployed into a new ring clears the fog out to it over a moment. It lies under the star-dots, prizes and pills, and never hides a star or star-dot; under reduce-motion it stays still.
- **P9e, Constellation reveal (2026-11-11):** a switch in ⚙ Design › Fun, off by default. On the Star view, two related star-dots both ignited are joined by a thin bright white line in a faint haze of the comets' sparkling dust, with small arrowheads at the end(s) it points to (brighter for a stronger relation, kept quiet so the pattern reads); as the second one ignites, the line draws itself from end to end. Not drawn under the relations layer, which shows its own. Tip: related star-dots placed at the same position in their categories' lists reveal their lines early. **The constellation view animates in:** every time Layer is turned to Constellation (by the designer or the player), its arrows travel from each star-dot to the one it relates to, then stay as the constellation view. The player's Layer toggle (Design › Player) now shows on the quiz map too, on a board with relations. The layer is not a bonus-ladder prize (one on a ladder from before is skipped). **Two-lane relations:** a relation each way between the same two star-dots is drawn as two lines side by side, each on its own right with its own arrowhead (the layer's arrows and the revealed lines). Hovering a relation line brightens it with the comets' sparkling dust drifting from origin to target; clicking it shows a label with who relates to whom and the relation's words (a click elsewhere, or on it again, hides it). The arrows no longer show a tooltip on hover.
- **P9c–d, your notes (2026-11-11):** a spin's outcome happens once the star has stopped (a prize won appears then). ⟲ Spin shows beside every star on the map as soon as a spin is claimed (no kite to tap), and any star spins. The player's 🎨 and 🌠 menus open fully in view. Prize comets (“A comet with your message”, “A comet the player writes”, each as often as you put them on the ladder) circle the sky for the rest of the session, in their own look (a star for a head, a brighter banner), several at once; a player's note sets off as soon as it's written and lasts this session only. Your other messages still cross once. “A comet with your message” is always offered in Add a prize…, with a box on its ladder row for its message (kept with Comets' messages as “With a ladder prize”; × takes both off). Cut out kite shapes starts with Clear. A comet is now a glowing head with its message inside a tail of white, sparkling dust (bright under the words, then thinning out; never taller than the head), each crossing at its own height and tilt from top to bottom, passing behind the stars. Comets now drift slowly, as if far off (40 px a second; a once-only message takes about 26 s to cross), and a prize comet rests out of view for 45 s between crossings; it still sets off as soon as it's won or written. Prizes now stay on the field until claimed and accumulate: each rung earned puts its own prize in the nearest empty hole to the star-dot that earned it, and the player claims them in any order. A completed category's kites keep their designs (with Kites: Designs): the design twinkles in place of the plain fill. A player's kite designs (painted in Play mode) now last for their session only: they show over the designer's, and a new session clears them; the designer's designs are untouched.
- **P9d (2026-11-11):** comets and messages. ⚙ Design › Fun › Comets: your messages, each flying once a session after N star-dots ignited, when a category is complete, now and then, or with the ladder prize **A comet**. A comet crosses the Star view behind the lattice, the message trailing as its tail in a banner; it waits while a box is open; under reduce-motion it shows still near the top. The ladder prize **Write a comet** gives the player 🌠: a note of their own that flies back in a later session, then goes.
- **P9c (2026-11-11):** Spin the star. **A spin of the star** is a prize (on the ladder as often as you like). With a spin to use, a tapped kite offers ⟲ Spin: its star turns like a pinwheel, each kite showing its outcome, and the kite that stops at the top under the pointer decides: a hint (the next question has one wrong choice crossed out), the next prize early, spin again, or nothing. Design › Fun picks which of the four are in play. After a moment at rest, the star turns back to where it was (the short way). Instant under reduce-motion.
- **P10, steps 3–4 (2026-11-11):** a content set is the library of items. It keeps a starter board: its items and questions, in their order and places, progress cleared. A new save on it (New empty map…) starts from a copy; a blueprint's board is its library. ⚙ Design › Board › **Update content set…** (with a confirmation) copies the open save's items, their order, the category names and the item fields to its content set; other saves keep their own, and the rest of the design stays in each save. A content set without a library takes one from the next save opened on it. Make a repo… doesn't ship the library twice. Help's Saves section explains the split.
- **P10, steps 1–2 (2026-11-11):** every save keeps its own design. A save carries a copy of every ⚙ Design setting (the categories' names and colors, the item fields, Stars, Questions, Look, Player, Fun, the screen's layout, the words), written into the save; a Design change never touches the content set or another save on it. A save from before takes its own copy of its content set's design the first time it opens, so it looks and plays as before. Duplicate… and Download… carry the design. **Undo is no longer a bonus-ladder prize**: Design › Player gives it for the whole session, or not.
- **P9b, part 2 (2026-11-11):** more prizes, none staged, in any order: the kite designer's **Cut out kite shapes** (Ink and Clear) and **Paint on kites** (Light and Dark, Lines, every symmetry), each built-in **palette**, and **Your own palette**. A new Player switch, **Palette (🎨)**, gives the player a palette menu that recolors their board only (Play mode, this session; locked palettes greyed with a padlock); their own palette is kept with the save. The player's kite designs always apply to every kite of a category. With the ladder on, the kite prizes and palettes are only ever earned (one not on the ladder isn't offered; 🎨 and 🖌 appear with the first claimed); with it off, Player decides as before. A prize now sits, smaller and zooming with the map, in the hexagonal hole between stars' positions nearest the star-dot that earned it (around the central star too, where its neighbors would be). The prize icon is a third of that size. The prizes are now only the Star view's tools (Undo, Kites: Designs, the layer, Swap, Rotate star-dots, Rotate kites), the kite designer's two and the palettes; one that can't work under the board's rules (moves under Independent, swapping with Kites lock, the layer in Quiz mode) isn't offered. On the quiz map a tap on a kite now selects it when the player has a tool for it (🖌, ↻, ⟳, Swap kite…), and Kites: Solid | Designs shows once there's a design. Design › Fun's ladder shows its × (take a prize off) without hovering; Add a prize… leaves out what can't work under the board's rules (instead of greying it). The Star view now opens on the central star and ring 1 (Fit too, until the board grows past ring 1). Kites: Solid | Designs is no longer a prize: it comes with either kite prize. With a ladder, every prize is offered on every board, whatever Player's Kite designer and Palette say. The ladder is reordered by dragging a row's grip (no ↑ ↓). Design no longer says whose settings they are (“These belong to…”).
- **P9b (2026-11-10):** the bonus ladder (⚙ Design › Fun, off by default). The designer puts some of the player's tools on a ladder, in order; in Play mode each stays locked until earned: every N star-dots ignited (default 3) earns the next, which waits as a prize (its icon alone, in a glowing badge) on the empty position nearest the center of the Star view. Clicking it unlocks the tool, with a burst and “Unlocked: …”. A new session starts the ladder over; Edit mode is never locked.
- **P9a (2026-11-10):** bursts and the supernova on the Star view. A star-dot that ignites bursts in its category's color (bigger at a Zenith); a star whose every star-dot is ignited goes supernova (a ring pulse, its kites flooding out), and the camera glides to it when it's near the edge. A switch in the new ⚙ Design › Fun, on by default; nothing under reduce-motion. They wait while a question popout or a star-dot's screen is open, and play as it closes; so do the map's own animations (a star deploying or withdrawing, star-dots moving), under every setting: the map keeps showing the board as it was until the box closes.
